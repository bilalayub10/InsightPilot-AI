Refactor the ChartPlanner service to become domain-aware instead of using generic heuristics.

Current problem:
- BusinessClassifier correctly detects the dataset domain.
- KPIDetector correctly generates domain-specific KPIs.
- However, ChartPlanner still recommends nearly identical charts for every dataset (e.g. Revenue Trend and Channel Distribution), making different business datasets look the same.

Goals:

1. Preserve the existing public API:
   plan(df, domain) -> list[dict]

2. Replace the generic metric/category keyword selection with domain-specific chart planning.

3. Create a chart registry for each supported domain:
- sales
- marketing
- finance
- hr
- inventory
- operations
- customer_support
- healthcare
- education
- generic

Each registry should define:
- preferred metrics
- preferred categorical dimensions
- preferred chart types
- preferred chart titles

Example:

Sales:
- Revenue Trend
- Profit by Product
- Orders by Region
- Revenue by Customer Segment

Marketing:
- Campaign Performance
- Spend vs Conversions
- CTR by Campaign
- Channel Performance

HR:
- Headcount by Department
- Salary Distribution
- Attrition by Department
- Employee Tenure Distribution

Finance:
- Revenue vs Expenses
- Budget vs Actual
- Expense Breakdown
- Cash Flow Trend

Inventory:
- Stock by Warehouse
- Inventory by Product
- Supplier Performance
- Reorder Risk

Healthcare:
- Admissions Over Time
- Patients by Diagnosis
- Length of Stay Distribution
- Readmission Trend

Education:
- Student Performance
- Attendance Trend
- Enrollment by Course
- Grade Distribution

4. The planner should intelligently match preferred metrics and dimensions to the actual DataFrame columns using keyword matching and synonyms.

5. If preferred columns are unavailable, gracefully fall back to generic heuristics instead of failing.

6. Generate up to four high-value charts that feel like recommendations from a business analyst rather than generic visualizations.

7. Keep the code deterministic, modular, and fully rule-based.
Do not use LLMs or external APIs.

8. Preserve backward compatibility with the rest of the analytics pipeline.

The result should make different business datasets produce noticeably different chart recommendations while remaining robust for unknown datasets.
